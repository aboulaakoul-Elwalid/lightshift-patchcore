Part E · The MVTec AD 2 submission

E1. What the benchmark is and what we send

MVTec AD 2 keeps the labels of its private test sets secret. You upload anomaly maps; their server computes the scores. Each category has two private sets: test_private (regular lighting) and test_private_mixed (mixed lighting conditions, the one most relevant to this project). The headline metrics are SegF1 (F1 of the thresholded segmentation) and AU-PRO0.05.

What score to expect, honestly

Expect a low score. The AD 2 paper reports PatchCore at roughly 4.7% AU-PRO0.05 on can and a mean AU-PRO of about 29% across categories at 256 px, and reports that working at higher input resolution helps a lot (tiny defects vanish at 256 px). Our own can image AUROC is about 0.45 (chance). We verified that this is not an implementation bug: a minimal reimplementation outside anomalib gives the same, ImageNet weights load correctly, and the median defect covers only 5.6×10−5 of the image. The submission is a baseline row; the project's contribution is the lighting-robustness analysis, not the leaderboard rank.

Rules that matter

  • 2 uploads per 7 days; an upload that fails validation still consumes budget, which is why we run the official checker first.
  • Do not publish private-test images or maps in public repositories. Our repo contains code and aggregate numbers only.
  • Calibration normals must come from the validation split (policy QR6); we followed it.
