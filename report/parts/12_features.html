A3. Deep features: what the numbers are

PatchCore does not look at pixels. It looks at feature vectors produced by a convolutional network trained on ImageNet (1.28M natural images, 1000 classes). We never train it: its weights are frozen. It is a fixed "lens" that turns local image content into vectors where similar-looking content is close.

The network and the tensors (our exact configuration)

stepshapewhat it is
input image, resized3 × 256 × 256RGB, normalised with the ImageNet mean (0.485, 0.456, 0.406) and std (0.229, 0.224, 0.225)
WideResNet-50-2, layer 2 output512 × 32 × 32stride 8: each cell summarises about an 8×8 pixel step; mid-level features (edges, textures)
layer 3 output1024 × 16 × 16stride 16: larger receptive field, more object-part content
layer 2 downsampled ×0.5 (bilinear)512 × 16 × 16brought to the same 16×16 grid as layer 3
concatenate along channels1536 × 16 × 16512 + 1024 = 1536 channels
flatten the grid256 patches × 1536 dimsone 1536-dim vector per grid cell: "patch descriptor"
Deviation from the paper, stated plainly. The original PatchCore also applies a 3×3 average pool around each cell (local neighbourhood aggregation) and an adaptive pooling that projects to 1024 dims. Our own implementation (src/lightshift/patchcore.py) omits both for simplicity and speed; the reproduction gate (Part D) used anomalib's full implementation and matches the published numbers. Our experiments therefore compare variants within our pipeline, which is what an ablation needs.

Why two layers?

Early layers see fine texture, late layers see object structure. Taking layers 2 and 3 balances locality (to localise a scratch) with context (to know it is a scratch on a bottle). Layer 4 is too coarse (8×8 at this size) and too ImageNet-specific.

Why ImageNet features help when lighting is unchanged

Two patches of intact bottle glass under the same lamp produce nearly the same descriptor; a crack produces a descriptor unlike any intact patch. Distances in this space are meaningful for the lighting the bank was built under. That qualification is the root of the whole project.

Sizes you can verify

bottle: 125 bank images × 256 patches = 32,000 patch vectors; keep 25% → 8,000 × 1536 floats × 4 bytes = 49 MB, which is exactly the size of app/banks/bottle.pt. WideResNet-50-2 has about 69 M parameters, all frozen, never touched by gradient descent.
